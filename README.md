## Selected projects

### [Soly Vie Real Estate Analytics](https://github.com/elantary11/soly-vie-real-estate-analytics)

An executive performance solution connecting revenue, bookings, inventory, collections, and customer conversion. The analysis separates the 90.2% booking rate from the 65.5% collection rate, values the remaining 52 units at 32.6M EGP, and turns those gaps into practical sales, collection, and inventory actions.

### [Rabbit E-Commerce Analytics](https://github.com/elantary11/rabbit-ecommerce-analytics)

An end-to-end commercial analysis across 5,009 orders, 1,850 products, and 793 customers. It compares revenue with profit, tests the relationship between discounting and margin, identifies high-value customers and loss-making products, and evaluates shipping choices through both customer preference and profitability.

### [Enterprise Data Modeling — Star Schema Architecture](https://github.com/elantary11/enterprise-data-modeling)

A before-and-after redesign of a fragmented operational model. Repeated entities and ambiguous relationships were reorganized into fact tables, reusable dimensions, a date dimension, and an order-status dimension. The repository explains the grain, relationship choices, validation approach, and trade-offs behind the analytical model.

### [Excel Business Analytics Portfolio](https://github.com/elantary11/excel-business-analytics-portfolio)

Three interactive Excel dashboards covering gaming sales, aviation bookings, and real estate inventory. Each dashboard connects KPI cards, PivotTables, charts, filters, and trend views to a specific business question rather than presenting visuals without context.

### [University Management Database Design](https://github.com/elantary11/university-database-design)

An end-to-end relational design that moves from business rules and ERD to relational mapping, normalization through 3NF, SQL DDL, keys, constraints, and a final physical schema. It demonstrates how a complex university workflow can be made consistent, traceable, and ready for application integration.
