### [Enterprise Data Modeling - Star Schema Optimization](https://github.com/elantary11/enterprise-data-modeling)

[![Enterprise star schema model](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/elantary11/enterprise-data-modeling/main/assets/after.png)](https://github.com/elantary11/enterprise-data-modeling)

**Domain:** Enterprise Analytics Architecture  
**Stack:** Power BI, Power Query, Dimensional Modeling, Data Validation

Redesigned a fragmented transactional model containing disconnected and duplicated operational tables into a cleaner, analytics-ready star schema.

**My contribution:**

- Audited the original model, relationships, filter directions, and duplicated entities.
- Established baseline validation measures before making structural changes.
- Consolidated operational data and separated business processes into fact and dimension tables.
- Implemented shared dimensions for date, customer, product, and geography.
- Applied a junk dimension to organize low-cardinality flags and statuses.
- Used factless fact and accumulating snapshot concepts for process tracking.
- Revalidated the final totals to confirm that model optimization did not change the business results.

**Result:** A more maintainable model with clearer relationships, simpler filtering, improved analytical usability, and a reliable structure for future reporting.
