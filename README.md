# Mohamed Elantary — Data Analyst

I build analysis that can be followed from the business question to the final decision. My focus is on trustworthy numbers, useful data models, and dashboards that make the next action clear—not on adding visuals for their own sake.

## What I work with

- Business requirements, KPI definition, data cleaning, validation, and reporting
- Power BI dashboards, DAX measures, Power Query, and executive summaries
- SQL, Excel, Python, relational database design, ERDs, and normalization
- Dimensional modeling, star schemas, data warehousing, and statistical analysis
- Data storytelling, stakeholder communication, and technical documentation

I am developing my professional practice through Data Analysis training at Route Academy and a Data Engineering foundation at Taif University. My portfolio shows how I apply these skills to real-estate performance, e-commerce, operational reporting, and database design.

## Tools

`Power BI` `SQL` `Excel` `Python` `Power Query` `DAX` `Data Modeling` `Data Warehousing` `Statistics`

## Featured projects

### [Soly Lagoons Real Estate Performance](https://github.com/elantary11/soly-lagoons-real-estate-analytics)

Executive real-estate reporting that connects unit status, the visual masterplan, sales value, reserved pipeline, available inventory, unit-type performance, and Arabic management priorities. The repository documents the decision logic for each page and records the monetary KPI definitions that still need reconciliation across views.

### [Soly Vie Real Estate Analytics](https://github.com/elantary11/soly-vie-real-estate-analytics)

An executive view of 533 units, 288.9M EGP in secured revenue, booking performance, collection efficiency, customer segments, and the remaining inventory pipeline. The analysis separates booked value from collected cash so management can act on both sales and cash conversion.

### [Rabbit E-Commerce Analytics](https://github.com/elantary11/rabbit-ecommerce-analytics)

An operational analytics portfolio built around 5,009 orders. It links revenue, profit, products, customers, discounts, and shipping to show where margin is created, where discounting becomes a leak, and which logistics choices protect profitability.

### [Enterprise Data Modeling](https://github.com/elantary11/enterprise-data-modeling)

A before-and-after modeling exercise that converts fragmented transactional tables into a clearer dimensional structure with fact tables, conformed dimensions, and controlled relationships for dependable analytics.

### [University Database Design](https://github.com/elantary11/university-database-design)

An end-to-end relational design covering conceptual ERD, relational mapping, normalization through 3NF, SQL DDL, keys, constraints, and a physical schema ready for application integration.

### [Excel Business Analytics Portfolio](https://github.com/elantary11/excel-business-analytics-portfolio)

Interactive Excel reporting for aviation bookings, real-estate inventory, and gaming sales. Each dashboard is built to move from a business question to a concise visual explanation and a practical next step.
